A game to play together on one phone

Looking for something the whole family can play on one phone? One More Chance is a puzzle you pass around the table: everyone fills their own 9×9 board with the same shapes, and whoever leaves the fewest empty cells wins.

Try it in your browser

How do you play on one phone?

Each round two shapes appear. The first player picks one and places it anywhere on their own board — shapes can be rotated and flipped — then passes the phone. A “Pass the phone” screen shows whose turn is next. Everyone gets the same two shapes, so the round is fair; the boards just grow differently.

How many people can play?

Two players are free. With Premium — one purchase, no subscription — up to six people can play on one phone or iPad. An iPad on the table works great for a bigger group.

Can everyone use their own phone?

Yes. Players nearby connect over Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi — no internet needed — and everyone moves at the same time, seeing the others' boards at the top. Only the person who creates the game needs Premium for more than two players.

Who wins?

Whoever leaves the fewest empty cells. If neither shape fits, you take one more chance: an extra shape just for you. A perfect, completely filled board is the dream.

How long does a game take?

A game is at most 20 rounds. Played alone it takes a few minutes; around the table it depends on how long everyone thinks — a turn is one shape. With Premium you can set a turn timer of 30, 60 or 90 seconds.

What age is it for?

The App Store rates it 13+ because of the optional chat with friends and the ads. The game itself is colorful shapes on a board: no violence, no time pressure unless you turn the timer on, and you don't need to read to place a shape — younger children can play next to an adult.

Is it free? Are there ads?

The game is free. Ads appear only between solo games — never in a game with friends, on one phone or several. Premium removes ads, adds six more themes and up to six players.

Do we need the internet?

No. On one phone, and on several phones nearby, the game works offline. Internet is only for the online tables, friends and the Daily Challenge ranking.
