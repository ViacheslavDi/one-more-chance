One More Chance

One More Chance: press kit

A calm, clever polyomino puzzle you can play together: fill a 9×9 board with shapes and leave fewer empty cells than your friends. One phone for everyone, or each on their own, with no internet.

Download press kit (zip, 16 MB) App Store

The game

Each round two shapes are revealed and everyone picks one for their own board. Shapes can be rotated and flipped. If neither fits, you take one more chance: an extra shape just for you. Whoever leaves the fewest empty cells wins, and a perfect board is the ultimate goal. A game takes a few minutes.

What makes it different

  • Play together. One phone or iPad passed around, or everyone on their own phone nearby over Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi, no internet needed. 2 players free, up to 6 with Premium. In a nearby game only the host needs Premium.
  • Daily Challenge. The same shapes for every player in the world, every day, with a streak, a shareable result and your place in the world.
  • Online tables. Up to 4 players, 30 seconds per move. While there are few players, tables include ghosts: recorded games of real people, played back move by move.
  • Playable with VoiceOver. The whole game can be played without seeing the board: the board speaks where the shape stands and whether it fits, and reads the free cells row by row. Reduce Motion is supported too.
  • Nine themes. Jungle, Candy and Neon are free; Ballet, Gems, Luxury, Travel, Deep space and Sweet shop come with Premium. Each has its own board, pieces, sounds and music.
  • Friends and challenges. Add friends by link or name, challenge them to the very same game, chat. Game Center leaderboard and achievements, iCloud sync.
